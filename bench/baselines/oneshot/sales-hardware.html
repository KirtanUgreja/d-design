01  —  Where the margin goes

Sixty ovens. Sixty different bakes.

The oven is the last unmeasured machine in your building. It consumes the most energy, decides the most quality, and reports nothing back.

01

Energy runs to the schedule, not the load

Preheat and soak cycles burn gas whether the deck is full or half empty. No one on site can see cost per tray, so no one can act on it.

02

Variance compounds across the fleet

A 9% swing in bake time between stores, shifts and ovens. The recipe is identical; the result is not. Consistency becomes a training problem you can never fully solve.

03

Waste is discovered, not prevented

Burnt batches come out of the oven and go straight into the bin. The correction always arrives one tray too late — and it is paid for twice.

The pattern Every point of variance shows up twice: once in the bin, once in the labour spent re-baking. At 60 locations, that is a fleet-level number, not a store-level one.

02  —  The product

An oven that hits the profile instead of approximating it.

Sense

Chamber sensors, sampled every second

Temperature, humidity, airflow and door state are read continuously at multiple points in the deck — not inferred from a single thermostat.

Control

Closed-loop gas and fan modulation

Kiln adjusts heat input and airflow in real time to follow the bake profile, load after load, instead of cycling on a fixed burner rhythm.

Learn

Every bake uploads a signature

Drift is caught in days rather than quarters. Faults are diagnosed remotely, and the fleet gets better as more stores come online.
