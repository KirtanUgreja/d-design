The challenge at 60 locations

At scale, small oven problems multiply into big operating costs.

Inconsistency
The same recipe bakes differently by store, by shift and by oven.
Energy
Ovens are one of the largest, least-managed utility loads in a bakery.
Waste
Every burnt batch costs ingredients, labor and shelf time.
Downtime
Each fault means a technician visit and an oven out of service.

Multiply each of these by 60 stores and they stop being store issues. They become a line on the P&L.

A 10-store paid pilot, measured against your own baseline.
