Business model

Food at cost. We earn on the connection.

Typical order

2 plates of lasagne · Tuesday 7pm
Ingredients (to cook, at cost)£9.00
Cook's packaging allowance£0.60
Potluck service fee (12%)£1.15
Diner pays£10.75

Diners pay a transparent service fee; cooks keep 100% of the cost.

Keeping food at cost keeps us on the right side of home-cooking rules, and it's why neighbours trust us. Potluck+ (£4.99/mo) waives fees and unlocks meal plans.
