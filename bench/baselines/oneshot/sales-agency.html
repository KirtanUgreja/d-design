The diagnosis 02 / 06

Your reputation is 140 years old.
Your brand looks like everyone else's.

01

Undifferentiated at a glance

Put your identity beside four competitors and it is the hardest to recall. The wordmark, palette and branch system have not meaningfully moved since 2009 — and neither has the impression they leave.

02

Fragmented in the field

Eleven customer-facing properties, three wordmark variants and no shared voice. Every project has rebuilt the brand from scratch, so the sum of the parts is smaller than the whole.

03

Invisible to the next customer

Prospects under forty describe the bank as "solid" and "old". The qualities that make you safe are precisely the ones that make you forgettable in a feed.

“The problem isn't trust. It's that trust isn't visible until someone walks through the door.”

The idea 03 / 06

Steady
is a strategy.

Every regional bank claims "local" and "trusted". Neither is a differentiator any more. Reliability — proven, visible and unmistakably modern — is. We make steady feel like momentum, not history.

Positioning

For people and businesses who need a bank that will still be there, we are the steady choice — built to move at the speed of today.

Rooted

140 years of local capital, told as a competitive advantage rather than a history lesson.

Responsive

A digital-first experience that answers in seconds — the modern face of a patient institution.

Unmistakable

A system distinct enough to be recognised in a screenshot with the logo cropped out. That is the test we build to.
