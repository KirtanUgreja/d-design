01 — The situation

Demand isn’t the constraint. Delivery is.

Basket’s order volume grew 41% last year. Delivery capacity grew 9%. The gap is showing up as long slot times, checkout drop-off, and a cost per drop that climbs every quarter.

01

Fixed cost per drop

Own-fleet routes only pencil out above 18 drops per hour of density. Suburban zones run at 6–9 — and still carry a full shift’s cost.

02

Peak windows squeeze capacity

78% of orders land in four evening hours. Fleet and drivers sit idle midday, then fail the window when demand spikes.

03

Coverage gaps cost customers

Outside the core zone, delivery time doubles and roughly 1 in 9 orders is abandoned at checkout. That’s demand we already have, going elsewhere.

04 — How it works

Four steps. No changes to Basket’s checkout.

1

Connect

Relay plugs into Basket’s order API and dispatch queue. Two weeks of integration work, owned jointly, with no changes to the customer app.

2

Batch

Orders are grouped by store, zone and slot. A courier sees one stacked route instead of eight separate trips across the same postcodes.

3

Deliver

Live tracking inside Basket’s app, plus photo and temperature-flagged proof of delivery on every single drop.

4

Recover

Failed drops, returns and substitutions are routed back within the same shift — not queued for the next day’s fleet.

Cold chain: every Relay bag is insulated and rated for 90 minutes, with a temperature flag on each drop. Existing grocery clients run at 0.6 breaches per 1,000 deliveries.
