The problem

Factories burn gas to make heat, then vent a large share of it out the stack.

~⅔

of industrial energy is heat

Steam and hot water for cooking, drying, distilling and cleaning. Almost all of it comes from fossil-fired boilers.

20–50%

lost as waste heat

Condensate, exhaust and cooling water at 40–90°C leave the plant unused every hour it runs.

30 yrs

typical boiler lifetime

Operators won't rip out working assets. Every boiler installed today locks in gas combustion into the 2050s.

The solution

Don't replace the boiler. Upgrade it.

Thermacore modules capture low-grade waste heat, lift it to process temperature and feed it into the existing steam system. The boiler burns less gas and stays online as backup.

≤40%boiler fuel displaced
<10 daysplant downtime to install
0changes to the process
01

Bolt-on, skid-mounted

Factory-built 1–3 MW modules tie into feedwater and low-pressure steam headers. No new boiler house.

02

Runs on heat already paid for

Warm sources give it a high COP. One unit of electricity delivers 3.5–5 units of heat.

03

Boiler as backup, never a risk

Controls hand load back to the boiler instantly. Uptime and steam quality are never compromised.
