What we missed

SOC 2 Type II slipped from September to November.

Slipped · two months · no revenue impact so far

What happened

The auditor's readiness review surfaced gaps in how we collect evidence for access reviews. We chose to fix the underlying process rather than produce evidence we couldn't sustain in the audit window.

Impact

No mid-size firm in our pipeline has made SOC 2 a gating requirement yet, so nothing stalled. We expect it to appear in larger procurement reviews, which is exactly why we're not rushing it.
