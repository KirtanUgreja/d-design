The problem

Documentation is the second job
physiotherapists never applied for.

A physio session produces assessment findings, special tests, outcome measures and a treatment plan. Today that becomes unpaid admin — usually after the last patient has gone home.

01

Notes follow the clinician home

Therapy notes get written in the gaps — between patients, over lunch, at 9pm. Quality drops the further a note gets from the session it describes.

02

Generic scribes don't speak MSK

General dictation tools capture words, not clinical structure. No outcome measures, no special tests, no plan — so therapists rewrite everything anyway.

03

Compliance stops pilots dead

Practices will not send PHI to a vendor without HIPAA and SOC 2. Most scribe tools stall in clinic procurement before a single therapist logs in.

How it works

Four steps, one 30-minute onboarding call.

01

Capture

Lumen joins the session on a phone, tablet or desktop in the treatment room. No new hardware, no headset, no separate dictation device.

02

Structure

Speech is mapped to physiotherapy note structure — assessment findings, special tests, outcome measures and the treatment plan.

03

Review

The therapist reads a complete draft, edits inline if needed, and signs. Nothing enters the record without clinician approval.

04

Write-back

The signed note is filed into the EHR the practice already uses. No integration project, no change to clinic workflow.
