The Situation

Every new source makes the loyalty dataset harder to trust.

Member data lives across a dozen systems that were never designed to agree with each other. The cost shows up three ways: integration time, pipeline incidents, and analysts who have to reconcile before they can analyze.

Fragmented sources

POS, e-commerce, mobile app, CRM, returns, fuel, pharmacy — each with its own schema, cadence, and its own definition of "member."

Change costs a quarter

Adding a single attribute means pipeline work, backfill, reconciliation, and a release window before anyone sees it.

Incidents erode trust

Every new source adds failure modes. When the numbers disagree, the business stops using them — and the team goes back to spreadsheets.

Governance bolted on

PII handling, consent, retention, and lineage are added after the fact — retrofitted into pipelines that were never designed for them.

The Platform

One platform, from raw source to governed member profile.

Strata is a single system of record for the pipeline lifecycle. Connect, resolve, govern, serve — configured rather than built, and versioned like the rest of your infrastructure.
