The problem

Green CI, red trust.

A failing build stopped meaning “something is broken.” It means “run it again.” Once a team learns to ignore red, every real failure gets ignored with it.

⟳

The re-run reflex

Engineers re-run instead of investigate. Failures get muted, retried, or marked “known flaky” in a comment nobody reads.

◷

Time nobody owns

Flaky tests are unowned work. Fixing one is invisible; ignoring one costs the whole team a few minutes, every day, forever.

◎

Release velocity

Teams add gates, manual approvals and retry budgets to cope. Delivery slows down to match the noise level of the suite.

14% of CI failures are re-runs

From Flakeguard telemetry across 84 customer organisations: the median suite carries 41 flaky tests, one in seven CI failures is unrelated to the code change, and each developer loses 3+ hours per week to re-runs and triage.

Product

Connect your CI. Flakeguard does the rest.

No test rewrites, no annotations, no configuration file. Flakeguard learns your suite from the runs you already produce.

01 — INGEST

Connect

One-click integrations with GitHub, GitLab, Buildkite, CircleCI and Jenkins. Every test result, retry and duration streams in read-only.

→
02 — DETECT

Model

Per-test statistical models over your history separate genuine breakage from non-determinism. No thresholds to tune, no owner to assign.

→
03 — QUARANTINE

Remove

Flaky specs leave the merge path automatically — with failure evidence, first-seen date and owner attached to a tracked issue.

→
04 — RESTORE

Return

Quarantined tests re-run in shadow mode on every build. When they stabilise, they rejoin the suite on their own.
