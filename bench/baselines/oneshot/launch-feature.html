How it works

Ledgerly does the matching. Your team handles the judgement calls.

1

Reconcile automatically

Transactions are matched against your ledger as the period closes, without spreadsheets or manual tick-marking.

2

Surface only exceptions

Anything that doesn’t match goes to a single review queue, so reviewers can see where attention is needed.

3

Review, approve, close

Controllers resolve the exceptions and sign off, days earlier than before.
