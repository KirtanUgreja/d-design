# Rubric

One rating per deck, blind. 1 is worst, 5 is best.

| Key | Criterion | The question |
|---|---|---|
| D | Designed | Does it look designed, or like a template or an AI default? |
| S | Says something | Would the audience learn and remember a specific argument? |
| V | Varied | Do the slides differ from each other while reading as one deck? |
| X | Distinct | Could this deck be mistaken for another deck on the wall? (5 = never) |
| U | Usable | Would you present or send it with two fixes or fewer? |

**Defects:** count every visible defect — clipped or overlapping text, an unreadable colour pair, a broken chart, an empty slot, a stray word. Any defect no metric flagged becomes a new metric.

Anchors: a v3 sample deck is a 1 on Designed. A deck you would present to an investor tomorrow is a 5 on Usable.
