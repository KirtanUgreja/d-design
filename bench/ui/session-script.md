# UI session script — phase 30

A 30-minute moderated session with one founder or salesperson who presents to investors, buyers or partners within the next month. Run it on the prototype (`cd web && pnpm build && pnpm start`), screen and voice recorded **with consent**. Five sessions set the baseline every later UI phase must beat ([../../docs/v4/plan/30-ui-prototype.md](../../docs/v4/plan/30-ui-prototype.md)).

**Say once, at the start:** *"We're testing the product, not you. Think aloud. There are no wrong moves. Some parts replay a real recorded run; I'll tell you if you hit one."*

## 1. Five-second test (2 min)

Show `/` for **five seconds**, then hide it. Ask:
1. What is this for?
2. Who is it for?
3. What would you do first?

Pass: they say "making a presentation / deck" and something about it looking designed or being fast.

## 2. Tasks (20 min) — observe, don't help

| # | Task, as said to them | Watch for | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You have a Q3 investor update next week. Start one." | Do they use the prompt or a chip? Do they read the placeholder? | — |
| 2 | "Answer what it asks, or don't — your call." | Do they find *Skip*? Do suggestions feel like their words? | — |
| 3 | "Is there anything in this outline you'd want to check before showing it to someone?" | Do they notice the YOU/WEB/AI marks? Do they open a source? | — |
| 4 | "Make it yours." (on Pick a look) | Do they shuffle? How long to pick? Do they understand a tile is a preview? | — |
| 5 | "While it builds — what's happening right now?" | Do they believe it's real work? Any "is it stuck?" | — |
| 6 | "Make slide 2 look different." then "Make the headline red." | Do they find the panel? How do they take the colour answer? | — |
| 7 | "You want to undo that." | Do they find Undo without help? | — |
| 8 | "Send it to your board, and present it on Thursday." | Do they understand Present vs Download to send vs PDF? | — |

## 3. Ratings (3 min), 1–7

- It was easy to get to a deck I'd use.
- I trust where the numbers came from.
- The look felt like mine, not a template.
- I'd present this with at most two fixes.
- I'd show this deck to someone today. (asked, not prompted earlier)

## 4. Close (5 min)

- "What almost made you stop?"
- "What would you tell a colleague about it?"
- "Whose deck is this — yours or the tool's?"

## Notes template (one per session)

```
Participant: role, company stage, presents to whom, how often
```
